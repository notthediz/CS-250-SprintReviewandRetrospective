# CS-250-SprintReviewandRetrospective

### How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating “user stories” help with this?

Interpreting user needs and implementing them into a program begins with understanding stakeholder needs and requirements and translating those broader ideas into smaller, tangible tasks by creating user stories with clear acceptance criteria. This helps guide the development work and ensures requirements are being met. During the SNHU Travel project, creating user stories helped establish expectations for features in the application, such as personalized destination recommendations. These user stories then allowed me to develop acceptance criteria for testing.

### How do I approach developing programs? What Agile processes do I hope to incorporate into my future development work?

I approach developing programs by first understanding the requirements and breaking down the development process into smaller tasks. One Agile practice I hope to incorporate into future work is regular communication between team members and stakeholders. This allows for feedback continuously throughout the development process and makes it easier to adapt when requirements change.

### What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?

In software development, being a good team member means understanding and fulfilling your role and responsibilities, collaborating with other team members, and being adaptable and open to feedback.
